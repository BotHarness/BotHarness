3D bot avatar — three.js, from scratch

One head mesh, one hair group, four cameras. The 2D version hand-projected every strand, faded and culled them against the head, and had to paint layers in a fixed order; here a turn is a rotation.y and the depth buffer does the rest. Hair parts (bangs / sides / back / accent) are curved shell meshes, so a style is a handful of numbers and the part list is still free to recombine.

LOOK FOR: the back view is real geometry, not a reveal trick · hair cast shadow on the face · one hair layer sitting on another · drag to turn the head · the HUD self-check
