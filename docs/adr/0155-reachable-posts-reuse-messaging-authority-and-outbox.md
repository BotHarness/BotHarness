# Reachable posts reuse Messaging authority and Outbox

For [#1115](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1115), an authenticated application may post to a group that its platform currently permits, without a saved Provider target or an inbound Source Event. The optional public Provider Service lists reachable groups and accepts a native conversation ID, authenticated fingerprint, cancellation signal and mandatory final beforeSend fence. It returns a checked native receipt. The first adapter slice is Lark; accounts without that capability retain the saved-target path.

Application-defined Messaging remains the sole owner of Binding, blocks, conversation entries and Outbox. Client Profile sends and Bot tools use the same command. A first post creates a normal implicit conversation entry in the existing database after reachability and current ownership checks; it never invents an inbound Source Event or Inbox Admission. Blocked conversations cannot send. Muting or holding reception does not remove platform sending permission. A native permission read happens again before every new effect, and the final fence rechecks current Binding, entry and Provider registration.

The default loop guard is 60 proactive attempts per rolling hour per Binding, recorded through existing Outbox attempt facts. Human changes to the app limit are persisted on the Binding and increment its revision; null turns off the guard. Replies backed by a Source Event do not consume this allowance. Concurrent reservation happens in the owning transaction, and the final fence checks the reserved count again. Unknown attempts consume allowance because their effect may have occurred. No second quota store or scheduler is introduced.

Accepted, refused and unknown results retain their existing Outbox meaning. A stable request identity deduplicates repeated submissions. A proven preflight refusal is failed; an uncertain result after a native effect is unknown and is not retried on restart. Cancellation and revocation fence unstarted effects, while an already returned native receipt remains evidence of acceptance. Generation 75 adds the nullable application post-limit column, with forward repair or restoration of a pre-upgrade backup as the downgrade path.

Lark reachability uses its native membership list and current chat moderation query. This first slice includes groups whose moderation setting permits all members; restricted moderator groups remain unavailable until the app's own moderator identity can be qualified. The platform remains the authority for membership and permission. Historical inbound traffic and metadata alone never grant new sending authority.
