---
title: 'DSH development context and BotHarness product language are now separate'
date: 2026-09-20
tags: [docs, architecture, dsh]
---

The DSH development section and installable `dsh-plugin-dev` skill now cover only DSH and Cordis terminology, decision paths, and implementation references. BotHarness product terms such as PersonaBot, Channel, Bot Inbox, Orchestrator Session, and Work Session remain authoritative in the root product glossary under `/dev`.

The focused BotHarness runtime reference has moved from `/dsh/bot-runtime` to `/dev/design/bot-runtime`. Existing English and Chinese URLs redirect to the new product-owned page.
